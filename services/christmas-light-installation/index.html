Holiday Lighting FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About DFW Christmas Light Installation

How much does professional Christmas light installation cost in Dallas, Highland Park, and DFW?

Most residential Christmas light installations in Dallas and DFW range from $450 to $950 for single story rooflines, $950 to $2,400 for two story brick and stone homes in Plano, Frisco, McKinney, Allen, and Richardson, and $2,500 to $8,500+ for Highland Park, University Park, Preston Hollow, Southlake, and Westlake estate rooflines with mature live oak canopy wraps. Every turnkey package includes custom cut commercial C9 LED lights, non damaging clips, timers, in season maintenance, and January takedown.

How do you attach Christmas lights to Highland Park slate, clay tile, or copper gutters without damage?

We never use nails, screws, or staples. Our crews use non invasive magnetic clips for steel trim, specialized clay tile hooks for Spanish and Mediterranean roofs, copper safe gutter brackets, and commercial shingle tabs so your roof warranty and historic facade stay 100 percent intact.

Why hire a professional tree service company for holiday tree wrapping in DFW?

Godoy's Wolves climbs, trims, and cares for 60 foot Texas live oaks, pecans, post oaks, red oaks, and Southern magnolias across all 19 DFW service cities year round. Unlike seasonal only light installers, our arborist trained crews know how to wrap high upper canopies and multi trunk specimen trees safely without spikes, staples, or bark damage.

When should I book my Christmas light installation in Dallas, Highland Park, Plano, or Frisco?

Peak installation dates in October and November fill up fast ahead of Thanksgiving week. Booking in October or early November guarantees priority scheduling so your home or commercial property is ready to shine the moment the holiday season begins.

What happens if a bulb goes out or a North Texas windstorm knocks a strand loose?

Every installation includes our worry free in season maintenance guarantee. If a bulb goes out, a GFCI outlet trips after rain, or high winds shift a clip, call or text us at (469) 301-6801 and our local DFW crew will service your display within 24 to 48 hours at zero cost to you.

Is post season takedown and storage included in your holiday lighting service?

Yes. In January, our crew returns to gently remove every clip and light strand, label your custom fit roofline harnesses by peak and tree location, and pack them for clean storage so next year's installation is even faster.
