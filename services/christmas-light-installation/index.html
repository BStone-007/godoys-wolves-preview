Christmas Light Installation FAQs

How much does professional Christmas light installation cost in Dallas, Garland, and Plano TX?

Most residential Christmas light installations in Dallas Fort Worth range from $450 to $900 for single story front rooflines and entryways, $900 to $1,800 for two story rooflines with ridge peaks and walkway stakes, and $1,800 to $4,500+ for custom estate displays featuring mature live oak canopy wrapping, wreaths, and backyard patio lighting. Commercial and HOA monument displays typically start around $1,200 depending on linear footage and tree count. Every project starts with a free quote.

When should I book holiday light installation in the DFW Metroplex?

Early booking in October and early November secures priority scheduling before Thanksgiving week. Our crews install residential and commercial holiday lighting across DFW from mid October through mid December, with takedown scheduled in January after New Year's Day.

Do you provide commercial grade LED lights or install customer owned lights?

We offer turnkey packages using commercial grade C9 LED roofline bulbs and sealed 5mm wide angle mini LEDs for trees and shrubs, custom fitted to your property for maximum brightness and weather resistance. If you already own professional grade lights in safe working condition, tell us during your quote so we can inspect them and price installation and removal accordingly.

How do you attach roofline lights and wrap large Texas live oaks without damage?

We never use staples, nails, or screws on your roof shingles, fascia, or tree bark. Roofline C9 strands attach with non invasive shingle tabs, gutter clips, and ridge clips. Because Godoy's Wolves is a full service tree care company, our climbers wrap live oaks, red oaks, and pecans with proper tension and spacing so the bark and branches are completely protected.

What happens if a bulb or section goes out during the holiday season?

Give us a call at (469) 301-6801. North Texas wind and winter rain can occasionally trip a GFCI outlet or loosen a clip. Our crew provides responsive in season maintenance to keep your display shining brightly through New Year's Day.

Is post season takedown and removal included?

Yes. Our holiday lighting service covers both professional installation and safe post season removal in January. We remove every bulb, clip, timer, stake, and extension cord cleanly so you never have to climb a ladder.
