Get It Right Now, or Pay for It Later

Almost every tree in Prosper was planted by a builder in the last twenty years, and most of them still have the problems builders leave behind: stakes and ties never removed, nursery twine still around the trunk, planting depth too deep, and two competing leaders that nobody corrected. None of those are visible problems today. All of them are removals in fifteen years.

This is the one place where cheap, early tree work genuinely pays. A formative prune on a young tree is inexpensive and permanently changes what that tree becomes. Common species we work on across Prosper: Live Oak, Red Oak, Cedar Elm, Chinquapin Oak, Bur Oak, and Crape Myrtle.

Common Questions

How much does tree work cost in Prosper, TX?

Small tree removals typically run $150–$500, medium trees $500–$1,500, and large trees $1,500–$4,000+ depending on access and condition. Stump grinding adds $100–$400. Trimming is priced per tree after Rafael sees it, because a crape myrtle and a sixty-foot pecan are not the same job. Every quote is free and on site.

Do you only do trees, or the whole property?

The whole property. The same crew handles mowing, edging, sprinkler repair, drainage, sod, beds, and hauling. Most people find us through a tree and keep us for the rest, which is cheaper for them than coordinating four companies and better for us than a one-off job.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Full liability insurance and workers compensation, with certificates available before any work begins.

How fast can you get to Prosper?

Estimates usually go out within the hour during business hours, and hazards get a two-hour response. Storm work runs seven days, and anything on a roof or touching a line is triaged ahead of routine jobs.

What happens to the wood and debris?

It leaves with us. Limbs, chips, and the trunk are hauled off and the ground is raked and blown before we go. If you would rather keep the wood cut to length and stacked, say so and we will do that instead.

Do you work with insurance claims after storm damage?

Yes. We photograph the damage before anything is cut and give you an itemized invoice your adjuster can work from. Emergency make-safe work usually goes ahead first and the paperwork follows.

Who actually shows up?

Rafael Godoy owns the company and quotes the work himself. You are not handed to an account manager, and the crew on site is his crew rather than a subcontractor with a magnet on the truck.

The builder left stakes and ties on my trees. Does that matter?

Yes, and it is the most common avoidable damage we see in Prosper. Stakes should come off after about a year. Left on, the tie girdles the trunk as it thickens and the tree either snaps at that point in a storm or slowly strangles above it. It costs almost nothing to remove them and it is worth doing this week.

My builder trees look fine. Is there anything to do?

Probably, and it is cheap now. Check for a second leader competing with the main one, twine still around the trunk, and a root flare buried under mulch. Correcting those on a young tree is a small job that decides whether you have a good tree in twenty years or a removal.

A builder damaged a mature tree during construction. Can it be saved?

Sometimes. The damage is usually root compaction or a trench cut through the root plate rather than anything visible on the trunk. We assess whether it is recoverable and say so honestly, because a tree that is going to die anyway should not cost you two years of care first.

Do I need a permit to remove a tree in Prosper?

Prosper regulates trees on some properties, and the rules differ between established lots and new development. We check the current requirement for your address during the estimate rather than assuming.

Do you handle new-build landscaping as well as trees?

Yes, and Prosper is most of why we have a page for it. Builder-grade lots usually need sod, beds, drainage, and an irrigation system that actually covers the corners, and we finish all of it once the house is done.
