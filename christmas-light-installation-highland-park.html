Highland Park, TX Holiday Lighting FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Highland Park Christmas Light Installation

How do you attach Christmas lights to slate, clay tile, or copper gutters in Highland Park without causing damage?

We use non invasive magnetic clips for steel and iron trim, specialized clay tile hooks that slip gently beneath barrel and flat tiles without lifting them, and non marring copper safe clips. Zero nails, zero screws, and zero staples ever touch your Highland Park roofline.

Can your crew wrap 50 to 60 foot mature live oaks and Southern magnolias in 75205?

Yes. Because Godoy's Wolves is a full time DFW tree care company, our climbers are trained to work inside mature live oak and magnolia canopies using bark safe tension wraps that expand naturally without girdling or scarring tree bark.

What color temperature of C9 LED bulbs is most popular in Highland Park?

Over 90 percent of our Highland Park clients on Beverly Drive, Armstrong Parkway, and Lakeside Drive choose classic warm white (2700K to 2800K) SMD LED C9 bulbs paired with matching warm white mini LED trunk and branch wraps for an elegant architectural glow.

What is the typical investment for estate Christmas light installation in Highland Park?

Custom two story slate or tile roofline outlines in Highland Park typically begin around $1,500, while comprehensive estate installations featuring multi peak rooflines, lit wreaths, walkway borders, and multiple mature live oak canopy wraps range from $2,800 to $8,500+.

Is January takedown and labeled off season storage included?

Yes. Every turnkey Highland Park package includes careful post season removal in January, custom labeling by roofline peak and tree location, and storage so your custom cut strands are ready for fast installation next autumn.
