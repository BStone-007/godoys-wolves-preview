Common Questions

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes — every residential project starts with a free, no-obligation estimate. We respond fast, usually within the hour during business hours.

What sod variety is right for my yard?

Bermuda for full-sun, high-traffic yards; St. Augustine for shade; Zoysia as the premium middle ground. We match the variety to your sun, soil, and budget — read our North Texas grass guide on the blog.

Do you handle small jobs or only full transformations?

Both. One-time cleanups, single-tree trims, and weekly maintenance are all welcome — no contract required.
