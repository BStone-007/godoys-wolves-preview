Common Questions

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Every residential project starts with a free estimate and no obligation. During business hours most quotes go out within the hour.

What sod variety is right for my yard?

Bermuda for full sun and heavy traffic, St. Augustine for shade, Zoysia when you want the look of both. Rafael matches the variety to your light, your soil, and your budget. Our North Texas grass guide on the blog walks through it.

Both. One-time cleanups, a single tree trim, or weekly maintenance are all fine, and none of it requires a contract.
