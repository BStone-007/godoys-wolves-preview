Our Stallions

Sport Friesian · Available 2026 Season

SB Snooby

SB Snooby is a registered Sport Friesian with exceptional bloodlines — his sire is a registered FPS (Friesian Paardenstamboek) stud and his dam a Tennessee Walker. The combination produces offspring with striking Friesian presence, flowing mane and tail, and the smooth, ground-covering Tennessee Walker gait. Well-mannered and athletic, with a beautiful topline and a willing temperament that passes to his foals.
