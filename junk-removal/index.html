Common Questions

How fast can you get here?

Same-day service is available for most jobs. Call (469) 301-6801 for a free over-the-phone estimate and today's availability.

How does pricing work?

Upfront pricing over the phone for standard items, or a free on-site estimate for bigger cleanouts. No hidden fees, no surprises.

Do I need to move anything myself?

No. You point and the crew loads, including hot tubs, appliances, and furniture.

What will you not take?

Wet paint, solvents, pesticides, asbestos, and medical waste, because those need licensed disposal we do not hold. Tell us what is in the pile when you call and we will say plainly what stays.

How is junk removal priced?

By the volume you actually fill in the truck, not by the hour. Rafael gives you the number before anything is loaded, so you are never watching a meter run.

Can you come the same day?

Often yes, and we work seven days. Same-day depends on where the truck already is that morning, so call early if you are up against a closing or a showing.

Do you do estate and foreclosure cleanouts?

Yes, including whole-property clears where every room has to be emptied and swept. We work with agents and property managers on tight timelines and can send photos as the job progresses.

What happens to what you take?

Usable furniture and appliances go to local charities, metal and green waste get recycled, and only what is left goes to the landfill. It costs us more than tipping everything and it is the right call.
