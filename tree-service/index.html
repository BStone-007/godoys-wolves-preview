Common Questions

How much does tree removal cost in North Texas?

Small trees (under 30 ft) typically run $150–$500, medium trees (30–60 ft) $500–$1,500, and large trees $1,500–$4,000+. Stump grinding adds $100–$400. Every job gets a free on-site estimate — see our full Dallas pricing guide on the blog.

Do you offer emergency tree removal after storms?

Yes — same-day emergency response for trees on structures or blocking access, with priority scheduling during active storm damage. If power lines are involved, call 911 and your utility first.

Are you licensed and insured for tree work in Texas?

Yes. Godoy's Wolves carries full liability insurance and workers compensation, and we'll provide certificates before any work begins.

When is the best time to trim trees in Texas?

Late winter dormancy is ideal for most species, and oaks should be avoided February through June to reduce oak wilt risk. Dead or hazardous limbs should come off any time of year.

Do you remove the stump after cutting down a tree?

Stump grinding is available as an add-on to any removal — doing both at once is cheaper than a return trip.
