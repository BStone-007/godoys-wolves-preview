Allen's Trees Are All the Same Age

Allen built out fast through the nineties and early two-thousands, which means most of the city's trees went in within about fifteen years of each other and are now hitting the same problems at the same time. Bradford Pears reaching the age where they split. Live oaks planted too close to the drive. Cedar elms that were never structurally pruned and now carry three competing leaders.

That timing is an opportunity if you get ahead of it. A structural prune on a twenty-five-year-old tree costs a fraction of the removal you will otherwise pay for after a spring storm. Common species we work on across Allen: Live Oak, Red Oak, Cedar Elm, Bradford Pear, Chinese Pistache, and Crape Myrtle.

Common Questions

How much does tree work cost in Allen, TX?

Small tree removals typically run $150–$500, medium trees $500–$1,500, and large trees $1,500–$4,000+ depending on access and condition. Stump grinding adds $100–$400. Trimming is priced per tree after Rafael sees it, because a crape myrtle and a sixty-foot pecan are not the same job. Every quote is free and on site.

Do you only do trees, or the whole property?

The whole property. The same crew handles mowing, edging, sprinkler repair, drainage, sod, beds, and hauling. Most people find us through a tree and keep us for the rest, which is cheaper for them than coordinating four companies and better for us than a one-off job.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Full liability insurance and workers compensation, with certificates available before any work begins.

How fast can you get to Allen?

Estimates usually go out within the hour during business hours, and hazards get a two-hour response. Storm work runs seven days, and anything on a roof or touching a line is triaged ahead of routine jobs.

What happens to the wood and debris?

It leaves with us. Limbs, chips, and the trunk are hauled off and the ground is raked and blown before we go. If you would rather keep the wood cut to length and stacked, say so and we will do that instead.

Do you work with insurance claims after storm damage?

Yes. We photograph the damage before anything is cut and give you an itemized invoice your adjuster can work from. Emergency make-safe work usually goes ahead first and the paperwork follows.

Who actually shows up?

Rafael Godoy owns the company and quotes the work himself. You are not handed to an account manager, and the crew on site is his crew rather than a subcontractor with a magnet on the truck.

Do I need a permit to remove a tree in Allen?

Allen regulates tree removal on some properties, and the requirement differs by zoning and by whether the tree is protected. We check what applies to your address during the free estimate rather than assuming.

Half my street's Bradford pears have split. Should mine come out?

Look at the fork. A narrow V-shaped union on a Bradford Pear over about twenty feet is the failure point, and once you see included bark in that crotch the tree is on borrowed time. Removal before it splits is cheaper and safer than removal after it lands on the drive, but we will tell you honestly if yours has a few good years left.

Can a structural prune save a tree from being removed later?

Often, yes, and Allen is the right age for it. Correcting competing leaders on a twenty-five-year-old tree costs a fraction of removing a forty-year-old one that failed. It is the single best value in tree care and almost nobody asks for it.

Do you handle HOA compliance trimming in Allen?

Yes, across Allen's managed communities, to the deadline and with documentation your HOA will accept.

Do you cover Allen lawn care and sprinklers too?

Yes. Weekly and biweekly lawn routes, sprinkler diagnosis and repair, drainage, sod, and beds. One crew, one number, priced by Rafael in a single visit.
