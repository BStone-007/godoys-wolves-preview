Post Oaks and Big Lots

Southlake sits in the Cross Timbers, so the native canopy is post oak, blackjack oak and cedar elm rather than the live oaks and pecans of the Dallas side. Post oaks are the least forgiving tree in North Texas about root disturbance. Trenching, a new drive, or equipment parked over the root plate can kill one two or three years later, long after anyone connects the two events.

The other Southlake pattern is scale. Lots here are larger than the inner suburbs, so a weekly lawn route is a genuinely bigger job and the difference between a crew that edges properly and one that does not shows up across a lot more linear feet.

Common Questions

How much does tree work cost in Southlake, TX?

Small tree removals typically run $150–$500, medium trees $500–$1,500, and large trees $1,500–$4,000+ depending on access and condition. Stump grinding adds $100–$400. Trimming is priced per tree after Rafael sees it, because a crape myrtle and a sixty-foot pecan are not the same job. Every quote is free and on site.

Do you only do trees, or the whole property?

The whole property. The same crew handles mowing, edging, sprinkler repair, drainage, sod, beds, and hauling. Most people find us through a tree and keep us for the rest, which is cheaper for them than coordinating four companies and better for us than a one-off job.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Full liability insurance and workers compensation, with certificates available before any work begins.

How fast can you get to Southlake?

Estimates usually go out within the hour during business hours, and hazards get a two-hour response. Storm work runs seven days, and anything on a roof or touching a line is triaged ahead of routine jobs.

What happens to the wood and debris?

It leaves with us. Limbs, chips, and the trunk are hauled off and the ground is raked and blown before we go. If you would rather keep the wood cut to length and stacked, say so and we will do that instead.

Do you work with insurance claims after storm damage?

Yes. We photograph the damage before anything is cut and give you an itemized invoice your adjuster can work from. Emergency make-safe work usually goes ahead first and the paperwork follows.

Who actually shows up?

Rafael Godoy owns the company and quotes the work himself. You are not handed to an account manager, and the crew on site is his crew rather than a subcontractor with a magnet on the truck.

Do you actually serve Southlake, or is this a wider DFW page?

Southlake is a real service area for us, on the Tarrant County side along with Grapevine, Keller and Fort Worth. It is a longer run from our Plano yard than a Collin County job and the quote is the same either way.

Why do my post oaks keep dying after construction?

Post oaks are the least forgiving native tree here about root disturbance. Trenching, driveway work, or even parking equipment over the root plate can kill one two or three years later. If you have building work planned near a post oak, get it looked at before the machines arrive rather than after.

Do I need a permit to remove a tree in Southlake?

Southlake regulates tree removal, and the requirement differs by zoning and by whether the tree is protected. We check what applies to your address during the free estimate rather than assuming.

Can you handle a full property on a weekly schedule?

Yes. Weekly grounds maintenance on larger Southlake lots, same uniformed crew each visit, with the tree work, irrigation and drainage handled by the same company rather than three.
