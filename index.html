Common Questions

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes — every project starts with a free, no-obligation estimate. Most quotes are delivered within the hour during business hours, and tree work gets a 2-hour response.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Godoy's Wolves LLC is licensed and fully insured in Texas, including liability insurance and workers compensation.

What areas do you serve?

The entire DFW Metroplex — Dallas, Fort Worth, Plano, Frisco, McKinney, Allen, and surrounding communities — plus Sherman and Grayson County for tree service.

Do you work with commercial properties?

Yes — commercial grounds are our specialty. We maintain properties for PetSmart, the Dallas Zoo, Harbor Freight, and Burger King, plus HOAs and local businesses.

Do you offer one-time services or only contracts?

Both. One-time cleanups, single-tree removals, and ongoing weekly maintenance are all welcome — no contract required to start.
