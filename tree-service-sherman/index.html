Built for Grayson County Conditions

Sherman and Denison see ice storms that snap limbs more often than DFW, dense Bois d'Arc trees, cedar encroachment on pastures, and large rural lots that need real clearing equipment. We bring the right gear and experience for North Texas conditions.

Common species we work on: Post Oak, Blackjack Oak, Bois d'Arc, Pecan, Eastern Red Cedar, and Hackberry. Rural acreage usually needs clearing, fence lines, and drainage as much as it needs tree work, and one crew covers all of it.

Common Questions

How much does tree work cost in Sherman, TX?

Small tree removals typically run $150–$500, medium trees $500–$1,500, and large trees $1,500–$4,000+ depending on access and condition. Stump grinding adds $100–$400. Trimming is priced per tree after Rafael sees it, because a crape myrtle and a sixty-foot pecan are not the same job. Every quote is free and on site.

Do you only do trees, or the whole property?

The whole property. The same crew handles mowing, edging, sprinkler repair, drainage, sod, beds, and hauling. Most people find us through a tree and keep us for the rest, which is cheaper for them than coordinating four companies and better for us than a one-off job.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Full liability insurance and workers compensation, with certificates available before any work begins.

How fast can you get to Sherman?

Estimates usually go out within the hour during business hours, and hazards get a two-hour response. Storm work runs seven days, and anything on a roof or touching a line is triaged ahead of routine jobs.

What happens to the wood and debris?

It leaves with us. Limbs, chips, and the trunk are hauled off and the ground is raked and blown before we go. If you would rather keep the wood cut to length and stacked, say so and we will do that instead.

Do you work with insurance claims after storm damage?

Yes. We photograph the damage before anything is cut and give you an itemized invoice your adjuster can work from. Emergency make-safe work usually goes ahead first and the paperwork follows.

Who actually shows up?

Rafael Godoy owns the company and quotes the work himself. You are not handed to an account manager, and the crew on site is his crew rather than a subcontractor with a magnet on the truck.

Do you serve Denison as well as Sherman?

Yes. Sherman and Denison are one service area for us, along with Howe, Van Alstyne, Pottsboro, and the rest of Grayson County. There is no separate trip charge for crossing between them.

Do you serve rural properties and acreage in Grayson County?

Yes. Rural lots and multi-acre clearing are a specialty, including cedar encroachment and fence-line clearing with haul-away or on-site chipping.

Can you remove Bois d'Arc (Osage Orange) trees?

Yes. Bois d'Arc is among the hardest woods in North America and demands the right equipment. Our crews handle them often.

Do you clear land and fence lines?

Yes, from a single overgrown fence line up to multiple acres. Grayson County clearing usually means cedar, mesquite, and volunteer hardwoods, and the stumps come out with the trees rather than leaving a field of hazards behind.

How do you handle ice storm damage?

Anything structural or blocking access gets priority, then the rest of the property once the hazard is gone. North Texas ice breaks tops out of trees rather than uprooting them, which leaves hanging limbs more dangerous than what already fell.

Do you charge extra to come to Sherman or Denison?

No. Grayson County is a service area rather than an out-of-area call, and the quote is the quote regardless of which side of the county line the property sits on.
