Common Questions

How much does tree removal cost in Sherman, TX?

Small trees typically run $150–$500, medium trees $500–$1,500, and large trees $1,500–$4,000+ depending on access and condition. Stump grinding adds $100–$400. Every job gets a free on-site estimate.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — full liability insurance and workers compensation, with certificates available before any work begins.

Do you serve rural properties and acreage in Grayson County?

Yes — rural lots and multi-acre clearing are a specialty, including cedar encroachment and fence-line clearing with haul-away or on-site chipping.

Can you remove Bois d'Arc (Osage Orange) trees?

Yes. Bois d'Arc is among the hardest woods in North America and demands the right equipment — our crews handle them regularly.
